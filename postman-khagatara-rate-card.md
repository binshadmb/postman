# postman.khagatara.com — Full Rate Card

**Pricing philosophy (two different rules by module):**
- **Print & Post, Cards:** price = sender's local reference price (what they'd pay at home) → feels light to them, India cost is a fraction → high margin (~3-8x)
- **Newspaper/Media Ads:** price = actual Indian newspaper rate + flat 15-20% margin. Indian paper ad rates (esp. Malayalam press) are already comparable to or higher than European local classified rates, so anchoring to "local reference" would underprice — anchor to real India cost instead

All prices below are draft/indicative — confirm exact India Post + paper rates at time of launch (both revise periodically).

## Module 4: Newspaper/Media Ad Placement
**Priced at actual India rate + 15-20% margin (NOT anchored to EU local price)**

Example — Malayala Manorama, Classified Text (10 words), by city:

| City/Edition | Actual paper rate | **Price charged (+18%)** |
|---|---|---|
| Trivandrum | ₹4,880 | **₹5,760** |
| Kottayam | ₹5,620 | **₹6,630** |
| Kochi/multi-district Kerala | ₹4,040 | **₹4,770** |
| All-Kerala + metros (Bglr/Chennai/Mumbai/Delhi) | ₹8,380 | **₹9,890** |

| Ad type | Basis | Margin approach |
|---|---|---|
| Obituary (text) | per word/line, city-specific | actual rate + 18% |
| Obituary (display) | per sq.cm, ₹340–2,650/sq.cm depending on city | actual rate + 18% |
| Matrimonial | per word/line | actual rate + 18% |
| Public/legal notice | per sq.cm, B&W ~₹360+ | actual rate + 18% + ₹150 format-check flat fee |
| Classified (general) | per word/sq.cm | actual rate + 18% |
| Display ad | per sq.cm, varies hugely by placement (front page, back page) | actual rate + 15% (larger spends → lower % is still fine margin in absolute ₹) |

**Service fee structure:** since ad rates vary wildly (₹250 to ₹15 lakh+), a flat % (15-20%) scales naturally — no need for a fixed-price table here, calculator pulls live rate + applies margin.

## Pricing Notes
- All prices shown in ₹; display dual currency (sender's local currency + ₹) at checkout, using live FX via Razorpay
- GST (18%) applies on your service fee component — structure invoicing so GST is charged only on your margin/service fee, not pass-through India Post/paper costs, where legally applicable (confirm with your GST filer setup)
- Floor rule: if EU local-reference price for any print/post/card item ever comes in under (India cost × 1.15), fall back to cost+15% instead of local reference
- Newspaper ad rates should be pulled live/updated quarterly — do not hardcode, since paper rates revise periodically
